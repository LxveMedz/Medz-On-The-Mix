Cancellation Policy
Overview
At Medz on the Mix, we value your time and ours. To keep sessions running smoothly and fairly for all clients, we ask that you read and understand the following cancellation and no-show policy before booking.
Cancellation Window
72-Hour Notice Required
All cancellations must be made at least 72 hours before your scheduled session. Cancellations made within this window will result in forfeiture of your deposit.
Cancellations Outside 72 Hours
If you cancel more than 72 hours in advance, your deposit may be applied as a credit toward a future session, subject to availability.
No-Shows & Late Arrivals
No-Shows
Failure to show up for your scheduled session without prior notice will result in full forfeiture of your deposit. The session will not be rescheduled without a new deposit.
Late Arrivals
Your session time begins at your scheduled start time regardless of arrival. Late arrivals will not receive additional time and the full session fee remains due.
Deposits
All deposits are non-refundable in the event of a no-show or late cancellation. Deposits go toward your total session balance, which is due in full on the day of your session.
Rescheduling
Rescheduling requests made more than 72 hours in advance are accommodated at no additional charge based on availability. Rescheduling within 72 hours of your session will be treated as a cancellation and a new deposit will be required.
Contact
Questions about your booking or this policy? Reach out directly at medzonthemix@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
